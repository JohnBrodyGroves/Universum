## Who is the Learner?
- The current learner we are focused on is the non-traditional adult learner working towards earning their GED/Highschool Diploma Equivalent.
- An intense focus on self-pace with the absense expectations.

## The Learning Loop
- Learn: Read short lessons about the one skill along with visual materials and examples.
- Practice: Answer questions and get instant feedback. No hint simply an explanation of the error and the specific skill that needs to be developed.
- Prove Competence: Competence criteria in Universum will be 100% for a skill across three consecutive days.
- Growth: Progression will unlock the appropriate next skill/skills for the loop to start again.

## Scope Focus
- The skills introduced in this beginning pilot version will be as fundamental, focused, and only focused on what will assist an individual preparing for their GED test.
