# Whole number operations

There are four operations: add (+), subtract (-), multiply (x) and divide (/).

Look for the words in the question that tell you which one to use:

- "in all", "total", "altogether": usually add
- "how many more", "left", "difference": usually subtract
- "each", "per", "groups of": usually multiply or divide

Example: You have 3 shelves with 8 books each. How many books in all?
3 groups of 8 is 3 x 8 = 24 books.
